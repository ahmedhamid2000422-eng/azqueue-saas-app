SMS Opt-In & Call to Action

How customers consent to receive text messages from AzQueue.

About AzQueue

AzQueue is a queue and appointment management service operated at azqueue.io. Service businesses use AzQueue to manage their walk-in queue and appointment bookings. Their customers book an appointment or join the walk-in queue through an AzQueue booking page.

Because in-person wait times vary, customers may optionally choose to receive text message notifications from AzQueue so they know when their appointment is confirmed and when it is their turn to be seen. AzQueue is the sender of these messages.

Step-by-step opt-in workflow

  1. The customer opens an AzQueue booking form, either directly by link or by scanning the QR code posted at the business front desk.
  2. The customer selects the service they need.
  3. The customer selects an available day and appointment time.
  4. Name, phone number, and email fields appear. The customer enters their details.
  5. Directly beneath the phone field is a separate SMS consent checkbox that is unchecked by default. The customer must actively check this box to opt in. The exact wording is quoted in full below.
  6. The customer submits the form. Checking the SMS box is optional — the booking is created and confirmed whether or not the box is checked. SMS consent is never a condition of booking or of receiving service.
  7. Text messages are sent only if the customer checked the box. Customers who do not opt in receive their confirmation by email instead.

Sample messages

  • "AzQueue: Hi Maria, you joined the queue at Az Tax Services. You are #A014 - 3 people ahead. Est. wait: ~30 min. Reply STOP to opt out, HELP for help."
  • "AzQueue: Maria, it's your turn! Please come to Counter 1 - Ahmed is ready for you. Reply STOP to opt out."

Opt-out

Customers may reply STOP to any message at any time. They receive one final confirmation and no further messages. Replying HELP returns contact information and assistance. Replying START re-subscribes.

Data handling

No mobile information or text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

This SMS program is used only for transactional appointment and queue notifications. It is not used for advertising, affiliate marketing, lead generation, or third-party promotional messages.
