These Terms of Service govern your participation in the Az Tax Services SMS text messaging program. By checking the optional SMS consent box on our booking or check-in form, you agree to these terms.

Program description

Az Tax Services uses SMS messaging to send you appointment confirmations, queue position updates, and service notifications. These are transactional messages sent only in connection with a service you have requested or are waiting for.

This program does not send advertising, affiliate-marketing, lead-generation, or third-party promotional messages.

Consent and opt-in

You consent to receive SMS messages from Az Tax Services by entering your mobile number and checking the separate, unchecked-by-default SMS consent checkbox on our online booking form or QR-code check-in form at azqueue.io/b/az-tax-services.

Consent is optional and is not a condition of booking an appointment or receiving service. You can complete a booking or check-in without opting in to text messages.

The checkbox reads: "I agree to receive SMS text messages from Az Tax Services about my booking and appointment updates. Message frequency varies (typically 1–5 per visit). Msg & data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel, HELP for help."

Message frequency and cost

Message frequency varies — typically 1–5 messages per visit or appointment. Message and data rates may apply. Check with your mobile carrier for details.

Opt-out

You may opt out of the SMS program at any time by replying STOP to any message from us. You will receive a single confirmation and will not be contacted via SMS again unless you re-subscribe.

To re-subscribe, reply START to our number. For assistance, reply HELP.

No mobile information or text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

Az Tax Services uses service providers, including AzQueue for queue management and Twilio for SMS delivery, solely to operate this notification program. These providers may not use your information for their own marketing.
