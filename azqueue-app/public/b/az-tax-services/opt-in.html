SMS Opt-In & Call to Action

This page documents how customers consent to receive text messages from Az Tax Services.

About the business

Az Tax Services is a tax preparation and immigration services business located in Aurora, Colorado. Customers visit in person for consultations, tax preparation, document review, immigration services, and follow-up appointments.

Customers book appointments or join the walk-in queue online. Because wait times vary, customers may optionally choose to receive text message notifications so they know when their appointment is confirmed and when it is their turn to be seen.

Where opt-in happens

Consent is collected on the Az Tax Services online booking and check-in form:

Open the Az Tax Services booking form

The same consent checkbox also appears on the QR-code walk-in check-in form displayed at the front desk of the office.

Step-by-step opt-in workflow

  1. The customer opens the booking form at azqueue.io/b/az-tax-services, either directly or by scanning the QR code posted at the Az Tax Services front desk.
  2. The customer selects the service they need (Consultation, Document Review, Follow-up, Immigration, or Tax Preparation).
  3. The customer selects an available day and appointment time.
  4. Name, phone number, and email fields appear. The customer enters their details.
  5. Directly beneath the phone field is a separate SMS consent checkbox that is unchecked by default. The customer must actively check this box to opt in. The exact wording shown next to the checkbox is quoted in full below.
  6. The customer submits the form. Checking the SMS box is optional — the booking is created and the appointment is confirmed whether or not the box is checked. SMS consent is never a condition of booking or of receiving service.
  7. Text messages are sent only if the customer checked the box. Customers who do not opt in receive their confirmation by email instead.

Sample messages

  • "Hi [Name], your appointment at Az Tax Services is confirmed for [Date] at [Time]. Reply STOP to opt out."
  • "Az Tax Services update: You're #[X] in line. Estimated wait: [X] mins. Reply STOP to opt out."

Opt-out

Customers may reply STOP to any message at any time. They receive one final confirmation and no further messages. Replying HELP returns contact information and assistance. Replying START re-subscribes.

Data handling

No mobile information or text-messaging originator opt-in data and consent will be shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing or promotional purposes.

This SMS program is used only for transactional appointment and queue notifications. It is not used for advertising, affiliate marketing, lead generation, or third-party promotional messages.
