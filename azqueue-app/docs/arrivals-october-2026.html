Both peak at 10am. Tuesday–Thursday is proportionally the more front-loaded day — 64% of its arrivals come before noon, against Monday's 56%. Monday spreads further into the afternoon, with a second bump at noon and again at 3pm.
So the pressure point is Monday 9–11, and extra help at 10am is worth more than the same help at 1pm on any day of the week.

What isn't charted, and why

Three obvious charts are missing on purpose.

Wait time, service time and total visit time. These depend on Call and Done being pressed when they happen. Median "service time" for a consultation currently reads as 1.5 minutes, so the timestamps are recording when someone tapped, not when a customer was served. Charting them would make a presentable picture of nothing.
Arrivals against completions. Worth drawing, but the completion times measured before migration 0065 included tickets the 30-minute timeout closed automatically with a forged completion stamp. Re-run it now the fix is in.
Anything per staff member. Staff were recorded on 9 of 272 tickets, and none in the last two weeks.
