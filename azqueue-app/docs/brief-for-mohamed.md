# Az Tax Services — what the queue system is showing

A short summary for Mohamed. October 2026.

---

## Busy days are not spread evenly

| Day | People per day |
|---|---|
| **Monday** | **24** |
| Tuesday | 10 |
| Thursday | 9 |
| Wednesday | 9 |
| Friday | 1 |

Monday is about two and a half times any other day. Friday is almost empty —
two people across two Fridays.

**Worth thinking about:** if someone works Friday, those hours would do more
good on Monday morning.

---

## Mornings are the busy part, every day

Most people arrive between **9am and 11am**, and 10am is the busiest hour of
the week. It is the same shape every day — Monday just has more people in it.

After 1pm it is much quieter.

---

## February is coming, and it is six times busier

From the old Qminder records:

| Month | People |
|---|---|
| January 2026 | 428 |
| **February 2026** | **977** |
| March 2026 | 850 |
| October 2026 | about 166 |

February is roughly **50 people a day** — and because Fridays are quiet, more
like **60 on the busy days**. That is more than twice the busiest day this year.

February starts in about **15 weeks**.

---

## Half of the tickets are never closed

Out of 502 tickets in three months:

- **202** were finished properly on the screen
- **237** were left open and closed automatically overnight
- **57** left or did not come
- **6** still open

Of the 237 that were left open, **about half were never called on the screen
at all**.

This does not mean those customers were not served. It most likely means the
work happened, but the screen was not used at that moment.

**Why it matters:** when a ticket is not closed, the system cannot tell how
long anything took. So right now we cannot answer simple questions like "how
long does a tax return take?" or "how long do people wait?" — and those are
exactly the questions worth answering before February.

---

## Two small habits would fix it

1. **Press "Call" when you bring the customer to the desk.**
2. **Press "Done" when they leave.**

That is all. Two taps per customer.

There is also a new **"Still open"** list at the top of the queue screen. If
tickets were missed during the day, it takes about thirty seconds at closing
time to say what happened to each one.

---

## One tool that is built but not being used

The system can take someone's documents, send them home, and keep the work in
a back list. When the work is finished, the customer gets an email to come and
collect.

Nobody has used it yet. In February, when 60 people may come in one day, this
is the difference between a full waiting room and a calm one — especially for
drop-offs and document reviews, which do not need the customer to sit and wait.

---

## What we still do not know

- How long a visit actually takes. We cannot measure it yet.
- How many staff are needed on a Monday morning. That depends on the above.
- Why some days are recorded well and others not at all. On 17 October every
  ticket was closed properly. On 15 October, with the same number of people,
  none were. Knowing what was different that day would help a lot.

---

## Questions that would help

1. On a normal Monday morning, is the queue screen open and in front of you?
2. When somebody walks up to the counter, what do you do first?
3. Roughly how long does each kind of visit take — a tax return, an
   immigration consultation, a document drop-off?
4. Would it be possible to close on Friday, or move those hours to Monday?

Nothing here is a criticism of how the office runs. The system was built
around assumptions that may simply be wrong, and the fastest way to fix it is
to hear how the work actually happens.
